Privacy Policy — Leo AI PM Threads

Last updated: 13 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Leo AI PM Threads automation (the “Service”), operated by the account owner of @leo.ai.pm, handles information when managing the account’s Threads presence. The Service is an internal tool for account management, content planning, publishing, research, and performance analysis; it is not a public consumer application.

1. Information the Service processes

  • Account and authorization information: Threads account identifiers and information returned by Meta’s Threads API, along with authorization tokens used to access permitted API features.
  • Content and interactions: posts published by the account, public Threads posts returned by keyword searches, and replies or other interaction data made available through the authorized API.
  • Performance information: account and post insights, engagement measurements, timestamps, and related performance data made available by Meta.
  • Operational information: workflow execution logs, error details, content drafts, approval status, and learning notes used to operate and improve the Service.

2. How information is used

Information is used to research relevant public conversations, prepare and review content, publish content when authorized and approved, manage replies where enabled, measure performance, troubleshoot failures, and improve the account’s content strategy. The Service does not intentionally use this information to identify sensitive characteristics of individuals or to make decisions about individuals.

3. Legal basis and purpose

Where data protection law applies, the operator will process personal data only where an appropriate legal basis applies, such as legitimate interests in managing and analyzing the operator’s social-media presence, or consent where required. Public availability of a post does not mean that all further uses of its information are unrestricted.

4. Service providers and sharing

The Service uses the following providers to operate:

  • Meta Platforms: to authorize access to Threads and provide Threads content, publishing, interaction, and insights API features.
  • GitHub: to store the automation’s source code and operational files and to run scheduled workflows. Authorization tokens are stored as repository secrets where configured and must not be committed to source files.
  • Google Gemini API: when enabled, to help generate or analyze drafts and replies. Relevant prompts and the content included in those prompts may be sent to Google for processing under the applicable service terms and privacy settings.

The operator does not sell personal information. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or to protect the Service, its users, or the public. Each provider processes information under its own terms and privacy documentation.

5. Storage and retention

Information may be stored in the private GitHub repository and its workflow logs, including structured files used for drafts, performance history, research results, and reply state. It is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for account management, learning, security, troubleshooting, and legal obligations, and is deleted or anonymized when no longer needed where reasonably practicable. Workflow logs and provider systems may have separate retention periods.

6. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information, including private repository access controls and secret storage for credentials. No internet service or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Access tokens and app secrets should never be published in source code, logs, or public issues.

7. International transfers

Meta, GitHub, and Google may process information in countries other than the country where the operator is located. Those providers are responsible for applying the safeguards described in their applicable terms and privacy documentation.

8. Your choices and requests

If you believe the Service has processed your personal information and would like to ask about access, correction, deletion, or another data-protection right, contact the operator using the contact details below. The operator will assess and respond to requests as required by applicable law. You may also contact Meta directly regarding information processed by Meta on its own services.

9. Children

The Service is not designed for children and is not intended to collect personal information directly from children.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the Service’s functionality, providers, or legal obligations change. The latest version will be published at this URL with its updated date.
